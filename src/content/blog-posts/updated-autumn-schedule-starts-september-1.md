The autumn timetable begins on Tuesday 1 September. Most of it is unchanged, because most of it was working, but there are a few things worth knowing before you book.

## What is new

Two evening classes join the timetable. A Wednesday Slow Flow Restore at 7.30pm, taught by Maya, for people who cannot make the daytime slow classes. And a Friday Kundalini at 4pm with Noah, which has been running as an occasional workshop and has earned a permanent place.

## What has moved

Saturday now starts half an hour earlier, at 9.30am, after a long run of people asking for it. The late Saturday classes stay exactly where they are; the shift-workers who come to those have been clear that the hours are the reason they can come at all.

## The full set of changes

- New: Slow Flow Restore, Wednesdays 7.30pm.
- New: Kundalini Yoga, Fridays 4pm.
- Moved: Saturday morning now starts at 9.30am.
- Unchanged: all Monday, Tuesday and Sunday classes.
- Unchanged: the late Saturday timetable.

## Booking and passes

Existing passes carry over with no action needed. Autumn booking opens a fortnight before the first of September, and the two new evening classes will be smaller than usual for the first month while we see how they settle.

If a class you rely on has changed and the new time does not work, come and tell us. We read every message about the timetable, and more than one of the changes above started as a note left at the desk.

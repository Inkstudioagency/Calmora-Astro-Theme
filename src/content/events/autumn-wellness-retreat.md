Autumn is the point in the year when most people quietly run out of road. This two-day retreat is built for exactly that: not an intensive, not a challenge, just a weekend of slow practice, real food and enough sleep.

Two nights at Sunset Grove, with a gentle flow each morning and a restorative session each evening. In between there is a guided walk on the Saturday, an optional workshop on breath and sleep, and a great deal of unstructured time.

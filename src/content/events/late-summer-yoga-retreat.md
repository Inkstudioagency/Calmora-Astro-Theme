Our longest retreat of the year, held at the end of the summer when the light is good and the water is still warm. Three days, two teachers, twelve people. You practise twice a day, eat together, and otherwise do as little as you can reasonably manage.

Mornings begin with a strong-ish flow before breakfast and end with a long yin or restorative session before dinner. Between the two there is nothing scheduled at all. Some people swim, some walk, several have been known to sleep for most of the afternoon. That gap is deliberate; it is the part people talk about afterwards.

Once a month, on the full moon, we turn the lights down and give the evening over to stillness. An hour of supported yin, then an hour flat on your back while Isabella works through gongs, bowls and chimes. Most people leave without saying very much.

The first half is a short yin sequence, four or five shapes held for five minutes each, entirely supported. The second half asks nothing of you at all: you lie down, get covered in blankets, and listen. Falling asleep is common and completely fine.

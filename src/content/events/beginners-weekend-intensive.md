Most people's first yoga class is spent looking sideways at the person next to them. This weekend is designed to make that unnecessary. Two mornings with Tessa Morgan and Maya Sinclair, covering the vocabulary, the etiquette and the handful of shapes that everything else is built from.

Saturday covers the ground: how a class is structured, what the names mean, how to set up a standing shape, and what to do when you need to stop. Sunday puts it together into a complete short practice you can repeat at home.

Ashtanga has a reputation for being difficult and closed off, which is a shame, because the sequence itself is the most teachable thing in yoga. This workshop takes three hours to walk through it slowly, with time to ask questions that a regular class never has room for.

Liam breaks the primary series into its four parts and teaches each one in turn: the salutations, the standing sequence, the seated sequence and the finishing order. You will practise everything at least twice, once slowly and once at count.

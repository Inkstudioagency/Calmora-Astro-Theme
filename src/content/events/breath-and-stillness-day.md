One day, two teachers, and a subject most classes only get ten minutes for. John and Isabella spend a full Saturday on breathing and meditation, moving between short practical sessions and long stretches of actually doing it.

The morning is given to breath: how it works mechanically, three practices worth knowing, and how to tell which one a given day calls for. The afternoon is given to sitting, with a yoga nidra after lunch for anyone whose body would rather lie down.

Power yoga is the hardest hour here, and Ava teaches it without any of the theatre that usually comes with that. Long standing sequences, few resets, honest core work and a close that is as long as the effort deserves.

## How hard is hard

You will sweat, your legs will shake and there will be a point around minute thirty-five where you would rather stop. Resting there is entirely acceptable and Ava says so at the start of every class; the practice is in choosing rest deliberately rather than collapsing into it.

## Strength, not speed

The class is not fast. Difficulty comes from staying in strong shapes rather than rushing between them, which is safer for joints and considerably more effective.

### Typical elements

- A brisk but complete warm-up.
- Standing sequences held for eight to ten breaths.
- Plank and forearm work in short sets.
- One inversion or arm balance, taught in stages.
- Ten minutes of cooling and a full savasana.

Not a first class. Come with a regular practice behind you, a towel and plenty of water.

This is the studio's everyday flow class, and the one most people settle into once they are past the beginner stage. Movement is linked to breath, the sequence builds and then releases, and the hour has a clear shape you can rely on however the rest of the day is going.

## The arc of the class

Ten minutes to arrive and warm up, twenty-five minutes of standing work that gets progressively stronger, fifteen minutes on the floor as things cool, and a real rest at the end. Whoever is teaching, that arc stays the same.

## Level and options

Intermediate means you know sun salutations and can move without needing every step named. That said, options are always offered in both directions, and taking the easier one is not a demotion.

### You can expect

- Sun salutations to build heat.
- Standing sequences repeated on both sides.
- One peak shape, introduced in stages.
- Hip and hamstring work on the floor.
- Savasana, at least five minutes.

It runs several times a week with different teachers, so if the pace of one does not suit you, try another day before deciding.

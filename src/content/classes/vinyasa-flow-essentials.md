Nina's essentials class is exactly what it says: the useful parts of a vinyasa practice, done well, with nothing added for effect. It is a strong class, but the strength comes from steady repetition rather than novelty, and there is no shouting.

## Building heat honestly

The class warms from the feet upward over the first fifteen minutes, then holds a working temperature for twenty, then cools. Because the build is gradual, most people find they can work harder than they expect without feeling wrecked afterwards.

## Early and quiet

This is a 7am class, so it starts quietly and gets going within a few minutes. Regulars turn up half awake and leave properly alert; several go straight to work from here.

### What you will meet

- Long standing sequences with few resets.
- Sustained core and shoulder work.
- One balance section, held longer than is comfortable.
- A short but genuine rest at the end.

Eat lightly beforehand or not at all, and bring a towel. Forty-five minutes is not long, and this class uses all of it.

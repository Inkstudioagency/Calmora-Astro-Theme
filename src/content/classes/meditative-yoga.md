This class exists because sitting still is a skill and most people are never taught it. Isabella starts with fifteen minutes of slow movement to take the edge off a restless body, then spends the rest of the hour on guided sitting, breath practice and yoga nidra.

## Meditation, without the mystique

No incense, no promises and no assumption that you should already be able to do this. Instructions are practical: where to put your attention, what to do when it wanders, how to sit so your knees do not object after ten minutes.

## The Saturday evening slot

Late on a Saturday turns out to be the right time for this. The building is quiet, the week is genuinely over, and people leave in no hurry at all.

### You will practise

- Simple seated movement to settle the body.
- Counting and following the breath.
- A body scan, lying down.
- Short periods of open, unguided sitting.
- Yoga nidra, roughly once a month.

Chairs, cushions and blankets are all available, and there is no correct way to sit. If your mind wanders constantly, you are doing it exactly like everyone else in the room.

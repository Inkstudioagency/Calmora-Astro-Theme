Ruby built this class around a simple rule: every shape gets three versions, and none of them is the lesser one. Whether you are on the floor, on a block or in a chair, you are doing the class rather than a modified version of it.

## Mixed rooms, on purpose

A regular Saturday morning here might include someone in their seventies, someone recovering from surgery and someone who ran ten kilometres before breakfast. The sequence is written so that all three are working at the right intensity at the same moment.

## Short by design

Thirty minutes is long enough to move properly and short enough to be manageable if energy or pain is a factor. It also fits neatly before the rest of a Saturday.

### Always available

- A chair version of every standing shape.
- Wall support for balances.
- Blocks and straps within reach of every mat.
- Permission to rest without asking.

If you have been unsure whether a yoga class would work for your body, this is the one to try first. Tell Ruby what is going on beforehand and she will build you into the plan.

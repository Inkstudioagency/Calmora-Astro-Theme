Thirty minutes, one idea. Zoe takes a single foundational shape or transition each week, strips it back to its component parts, and rebuilds it with the class. It is a lunchtime class for people who already practise regularly and want to spend their half hour going deeper rather than wider.

## Why so short

Because focused work does not need an hour. Without a warm-up to build and a long rest to close, thirty minutes is enough to do real work on one thing, and short enough to fit into a working day without changing your afternoon.

## Not a beginner class

The label says Expert and it means it. You should have a stable practice, know the standard vocabulary and be able to warm yourself up. If you are newer, take Foundations' longer sibling classes first and come back to this one in a few months.

### Recent themes

- Loading the wrist safely in arm balances.
- The step-through from downward dog.
- Hip external rotation without gripping the glutes.
- Breathing through inversions rather than holding on.

Come already warm if you can, and expect to leave with one thing to practise for the rest of the week.

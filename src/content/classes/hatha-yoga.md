Hatha is the parent of most of the other classes on this timetable. It works one posture at a time, with a pause between each, so you can set a shape up properly, stay in it and then leave it deliberately. There is no flowing from one thing to the next and no music to keep up with.

## Alignment is the subject

Michael came to teaching from physiotherapy, and this class reflects it. He is specific about feet, pelvis and shoulder position, and he explains what a cue is protecting rather than simply issuing it. If you have a knee, back or shoulder that needs attention, tell him at the start.

## Breath and stillness

Each posture is held for five to eight breaths, which is long enough for the initial effort to settle and something quieter to appear underneath it. The last fifteen minutes are given to seated breathing and rest.

### A typical class covers

- Standing postures, held and repeated on both sides.
- One balance, with the wall available.
- A gentle backbend and its counter-shape.
- Seated forward folds and twists.
- Ten minutes of pranayama and savasana.

Good for beginners who want to understand what they are doing, and for experienced practitioners who have been moving fast for years.

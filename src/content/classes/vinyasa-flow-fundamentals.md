Ella teaches the fundamentals as if they were the whole practice, on the grounds that they mostly are. The sequence barely changes from week to week. What changes is you, and the point of the class is to be there often enough to notice.

## Repetition as the method

A shape you meet once is a novelty. A shape you meet forty times becomes a place you can think in. This class keeps the vocabulary small and the attention high, which is far harder than it sounds and much more useful than a new sequence every Friday.

## Breath first

Every section is timed by breath rather than by count, so the pace is set by the room rather than the clock. If your breath gets ragged, that is the instruction to do less. Ella will say so out loud, more than once.

### The recurring shapes

- Sun salutation A and B, unhurried.
- Warrior II and its side-bending relatives.
- One standing balance, held for eight breaths.
- A seated forward fold and one twist.
- Savasana, five full minutes, no exceptions.

Come weekly for a month if you can. This class rewards attendance more than effort, and the difference is usually obvious by the fourth visit.

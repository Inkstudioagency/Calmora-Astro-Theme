This is the class for people who like to sweat and then lie very still. Sophie sequences it like a training session: a proper warm-up, a hard middle, a taper and a genuine recovery, all in an hour.

## Built for people who train

A lot of the room runs, cycles or lifts, and the sequence is written with those bodies in mind. There is deliberate work for hip flexors, thoracic rotation, ankles and shoulders, which is where most endurance athletes get stuck.

## The last fifteen minutes matter

Sophie will not shorten the close, however good the flow felt. Long supine shapes and a full savasana are the part that makes the rest of it useful, and skipping out early is the one thing she will actually mention.

### Expect

- Twenty minutes of building standing work.
- A demanding middle section with limited rests.
- Hip and hamstring work on the floor.
- Ten minutes of recovery shapes.
- Savasana with the lights down.

Bring a towel and more water than you think you need. It is a hard hour, but it is not a competitive one.

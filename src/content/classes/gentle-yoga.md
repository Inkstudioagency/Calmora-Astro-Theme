Emily's gentle class moves slowly, stays close to the ground and asks nothing that would leave you sore the next day. It is popular with people returning after injury or illness, people in their seventies and eighties, and anyone who simply wants an hour of movement without effort.

## Small range, real benefit

Most of the class works within a comfortable range of motion. That is not a compromise: joints do most of their maintenance in the middle of their range, and moving there often is worth more than occasionally forcing the ends.

## A chair is always there

Every shape has a seated version, and using it is entirely ordinary in this room. Nobody is keeping score and Emily is unfailingly patient with questions.

### The hour includes

- Seated warm-up for wrists, ankles and neck.
- Standing balance work with a chair or wall.
- Gentle spinal movement in all directions.
- Floor or chair-based stretching.
- A short guided rest.

If it has been years, or if it has never been, this is a comfortable place to start. Come a few minutes early and tell Emily what you would like to be careful with.

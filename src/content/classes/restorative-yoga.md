Restorative is not a gentle version of a yoga class; it is a different thing altogether. You will meet perhaps five shapes in an hour, each one built with bolsters and blankets so completely that your body has nothing to hold. Then you stay there, and the nervous system does the rest.

## Why it works

When a shape is properly supported, the muscles stop bracing. Breathing slows, the heart rate follows and the body drops into the state it repairs in. Ten minutes of that is worth a great deal more than an hour of trying to relax.

## What to expect

Dim light, few instructions, long silences. Sophie sets each shape up carefully and then leaves the room alone. Some people sleep. That is fine, and fairly common.

### Who this is for

- Anyone running on very little sleep.
- People recovering from illness, injury or surgery.
- Anyone whose week has been louder than they would like.
- Strong practitioners who never stop.
- Complete beginners; no experience is needed at all.

Come in warm, comfortable clothes and bring extra layers. You will not sweat, and you will get cold if you do not plan for it.

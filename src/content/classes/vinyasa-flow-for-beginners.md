Tessa teaches beginners almost exclusively and treats it as the most interesting work in the building. This class introduces the standard vinyasa vocabulary in a deliberate order, so that by the end of a few weeks you can walk into any flow class in the city and know what is being asked of you.

## How and why

Most beginner classes explain how to make a shape. This one also explains why it is being asked for, what it is meant to do and what it should not feel like. That extra layer takes a few more minutes and saves a great deal of confusion later.

## Steady group

People tend to arrive at this class in small clusters and stay for a term. It is friendly without being effortful, and Tessa remembers names, which matters more than anyone admits.

### Over a term you will learn

- The full sun salutation, broken into four teachable parts.
- How to set up a standing shape from the feet up.
- What to do with your hands and shoulders in a plank.
- Two breathing patterns and when each is useful.
- How to rest well at the end.

No experience required, no equipment needed, and no expectation that you will keep up with anyone.

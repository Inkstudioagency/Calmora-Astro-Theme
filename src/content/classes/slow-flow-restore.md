Half of this class moves and half of it does not. Maya opens with twenty minutes of slow standing work, enough to warm the hips and shoulders, then brings the class to the floor for supported shapes held long enough to actually let go. It is a small, kind class and people tend to leave quieter than they arrived.

## Who it suits

People who sit all day. People who have been told to relax and have not found that instruction useful. People coming back after a break, an illness or a long stretch of not much movement. It is also one of the best first classes on the timetable, because the pace never gets away from you.

## Props do the work

Bolsters, blankets and blocks are used throughout, not as a concession but as the point. A shape supported well can be held for five minutes; the same shape unsupported lasts thirty seconds and does much less.

### In a typical class

- Ten minutes of breath and simple floor movement.
- Twenty minutes of slow standing shapes.
- Ten minutes of supported reclined work.
- A five-minute rest with a blanket.

Bring socks and a jumper for the end. The room cools as you stop moving, and being cold is the fastest way to undo a good savasana.

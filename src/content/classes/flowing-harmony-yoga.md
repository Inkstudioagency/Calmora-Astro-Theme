This is a flowing class built around transitions rather than postures. Lila is less interested in how deep you get into a shape than in how you travel from one shape into the next, which is where most of the strength and nearly all of the grace lives.

## The sequence

Each month the class works with one transition family, returning to it every week from a slightly different angle. Over four weeks something that felt impossible in week one usually becomes ordinary. The rest of the class is a steady, warming flow with a clear arc and a proper rest at the end.

## What is assumed

You should be comfortable with sun salutations and know your way around a lunge and a downward dog. You do not need to be strong, flexible or fast; you need to be willing to repeat things.

### You will work on

- Moving through the feet rather than landing on them.
- Keeping the breath even while the shapes change.
- Shoulder mechanics in weight-bearing positions.
- Finding the pause at the top and bottom of each breath.

Forty-five minutes moves quickly here. Come with a little warmth in your body if you can, and stay for the last five minutes, which is where the class actually lands.

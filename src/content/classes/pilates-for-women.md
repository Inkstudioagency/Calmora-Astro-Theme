This is an hour of mat Pilates built around the things that actually change how a week feels: a core that works with your breath, a pelvic floor that is neither ignored nor over-gripped, and a spine that can move in every direction it was designed for. John keeps the groups small so that everyone gets seen.

## What Pilates is doing

Pilates trains control rather than range. The movements are small, repeatable and specific, and the work happens in how precisely you do them rather than how far you go. That makes it unusually good for anyone rebuilding strength after pregnancy, surgery or a long stretch of sitting.

## Breath and the pelvic floor

A quarter of the class is spent on breathing, because the diaphragm and the pelvic floor move together whether or not you are paying attention. Learning to feel that relationship is the single most useful thing most people take from this class.

### A typical hour

- Fifteen minutes of breath and alignment on the mat.
- Core sequences built in progressions of three.
- Side-lying hip and glute work.
- Spinal articulation, forward, back and rotating.
- A short standing section to carry it into the day.

Wear something you can move in and bring socks with grip if you have them. Tell John before class if you are pregnant, recently postnatal or managing an injury, and he will adjust the plan rather than ask you to sit anything out.

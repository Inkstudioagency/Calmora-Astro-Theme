Vinyasa means linking movement to breath, and this class is where that link gets built. We work with a small set of shapes, repeated often enough that your body starts to recognise them, and we stop as often as we move. If you have been meaning to try yoga for a while and keep putting it off, this is the hour to come to.

## What the hour looks like

We begin sitting, for five minutes or so, just noticing how the breath is arriving. Then a slow warm-up on hands and knees, a few rounds of a simple standing sequence, and a long settle at the end. Nothing is fast. Clara names each shape twice, once in English and once in Sanskrit, so the vocabulary becomes familiar without anyone having to memorise it.

## What you need to bring

Comfortable clothes you can move in and a water bottle. Mats, blocks and straps are all here. Arrive five or ten minutes early on your first visit so you can say hello and find a spot you like.

### Good to know

- No previous yoga experience is assumed at any point.
- Every shape is offered with a lower and a higher option.
- You can rest whenever you want to; nobody will comment.
- Hands-on adjustment is offered, never assumed.
- The room is warm but not hot.

Most people find the first class is the hardest and the second is easy. Come twice before you decide whether it suits you.

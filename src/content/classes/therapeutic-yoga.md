Therapeutic yoga is for bodies that need the practice built around them rather than the other way round. The groups are deliberately small, the pace is slow, and nearly everything is adapted. Many people come here alongside physiotherapy or after being told by a clinician that gentle movement would help.

## Before you start

Emma will ask about your history, your current symptoms and what makes them worse, and she will keep asking as things change. That conversation is part of the class, not an administrative preliminary.

## What the hour is like

Mostly floor and chair-based work with a lot of props, focused on joint care, gentle strengthening and breathing. Nothing is held to the point of strain and nothing is pushed for the sake of progress.

### Commonly worked with

- Persistent lower back and neck pain.
- Recovery from surgery or a long illness.
- Joint conditions and reduced mobility.
- Fatigue and sleep problems.
- Returning to movement after a long gap.

This is not a substitute for medical care and Emma will say so. It sits alongside it, and works best when your clinician knows you are coming.

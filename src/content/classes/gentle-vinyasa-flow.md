Gentle does not mean easy here, it means unhurried. The sequence is a full vinyasa with the pace taken down, so you have time to feel where your weight is before you move it somewhere else. Maya teaches it for people who like flow classes but find most of them too fast to learn anything from.

## Slower on purpose

Holding a shape for five breaths instead of one changes what it asks of you. The muscular work goes up, the momentum goes down, and you get honest information about what your hips and shoulders are actually doing. Most people find this harder than they expected and more useful than they expected.

## Questions welcome

There is deliberate space in this class to stop and ask. If something hurts, or a shape has never made sense, say so; the answer is usually interesting to the whole room.

### Typical structure

- Breath and mobility on the floor.
- A standing sequence built in three parts.
- One longer balance or hip section.
- Supine twists and a proper savasana.

Fifty minutes, mid-morning, and you can get on with your day afterwards without needing a nap.
